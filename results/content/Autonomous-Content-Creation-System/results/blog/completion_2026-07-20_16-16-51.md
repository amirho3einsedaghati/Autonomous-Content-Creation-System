---

## 1. Agentic Workflows: The Next Leap in Automation

**Autonomous agents, not scripted scripts.** Traditional RPA or rule‑based automation follows a fixed path. Agentic workflows are powered by AI models that can **make decisions, adapt on the fly, and coordinate with other agents**—just like a human would, but at machine speed.

- **Limited supervision model.** These agents are designed to hit specific goals with **little human guidance**. Instead of feeding endless data sets, you tell the agent *what* you want to achieve, and it figures out *how* to get there.
- **Human‑like decision making.** The core of an agent is a machine‑learning model that mimics human reasoning, allowing it to **solve problems in real time** and interact naturally with users and systems.
- **Self‑orchestrating teams.** Multiple agents can collaborate, delegating tasks, sharing context, and adjusting strategies without a central controller.

In short, agentic AI turns static automation into a living, breathing process that can handle complexity, variability, and scale—exactly the kind of agility modern enterprises need.

## 3. Your Path to Production‑Grade Agentic Systems

Moving from concept to production isn’t a sprint, but IBM’s event provides a **clear transition strategy** for enterprises at every stage.

- **Start with a pilot that defines a concrete business goal.** Use watsonx Orchestrate to prototype a small team of agents that handle a high‑impact process (e.g., invoice processing or customer support routing).
- **Leverage Confluent for real‑time data feeds.** Connect your operational data streams so agents can react to live events—like a sudden surge in support tickets—without manual intervention.
- **Adopt the Concert platform for governance and scaling.** As your agentic workflows grow, Concert helps you enforce policies, track performance, and ensure compliance across hybrid environments.
- **Iterate and evolve.** Because agents operate with limited supervision, you can continuously feed them new data, adjust objectives, and watch them improve autonomously.

By following this roadmap, you’ll be able to **exit the AI pilot phase** and enter the agentic era with confidence, turning autonomous AI from a strategic advantage into a core operational capability.
